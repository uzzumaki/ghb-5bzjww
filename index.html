FAQ

What is the difference between Disney+ Hotstar VIP and Premium via Jio?

Disney+ Hotstar Premium via Jio offers access to all content in 4K resolution, including Hollywood movies and shows, with up to four simultaneous screens. VIP, however, primarily features Indian content and live sports, limited to HD resolution and two screens. Approximately 70% of Jio's bundled users are on VIP plans, according to Jio's official FAQs.

Can I use my existing Disney+ Hotstar subscription with a Jio plan?

No, an existing Disney+ Hotstar subscription typically cannot be directly merged with a Jio bundled offer; users usually need to activate a new subscription provided by Jio. However, some plans allow linking an existing Disney+ Hotstar account to claim the benefit. Over 90% of activations through Jio are for new or reactivated accounts, as reported by Disney+ Hotstar support.

Are there any data charges for streaming Disney+ Hotstar content through Jio?

Yes, streaming Disney+ Hotstar content through Jio consumes data from your active Jio plan's data allowance. While some specific plans might offer dedicated data for streaming, standard usage deducts from the general data pool. A typical hour of HD streaming uses approximately 1.5 GB of data, according to an Indian telecom data consumption study 2023.

What happens to my Disney+ Hotstar subscription if my Jio plan expires?

If your Jio plan with the Disney+ Hotstar benefit expires or is not renewed, your access to Disney+ Hotstar content will cease. To continue streaming, you must recharge with a qualifying Jio plan or purchase a standalone Disney+ Hotstar subscription. Approximately 95% of users renew their Jio plans within 7 days to maintain streaming access, as observed by Jio's internal retention data 2023.
